Block Puzzle Master is developed by Saber Barghighach ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game.

Block Puzzle Master does not require users to create an account.

The game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • Mission and achievement progress.
  • Sound and vibration preferences.

This information is stored locally to provide the game's features and functionality.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information, including:

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies.
